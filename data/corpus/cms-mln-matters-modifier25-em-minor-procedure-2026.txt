Background. Payers continue to scrutinize claims that pair an Evaluation
and Management service with a minor procedure on the same date. Modifier
25 is the standard mechanism to indicate a significant, separately
identifiable E/M service. Practices billing Modifier 25 on more than 50
percent of procedural claims have historically been subject to Pre-
Payment Reviews.

Documentation requirements. The E/M note must demonstrate a distinct
chief complaint, separate exam findings, and an independent medical
decision. A note that reads as procedure-only workup will not support
the modifier under audit.

Common pitfalls. Submitting Modifier 25 when the chief complaint is the
procedure indication. Documenting an exam limited to the procedure site.
Documenting medical decision making that addresses only the procedure.
Each of these failure modes is independently sufficient to disallow the
E/M service.
