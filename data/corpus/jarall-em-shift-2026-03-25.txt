The 2026 E/M reimbursement shift increases scrutiny on documentation
that supports a separately identifiable E/M service. The Centers for
Medicare and Medicaid Services along with major commercial payers
continue to deploy AI-driven claim review for Modifier 25 utilization.

Practical implications for podiatry practices:

- Documentation defensibility is now a revenue cycle issue. A note that
  cannot defend an attached Modifier 25 will lose the E/M reimbursement
  on appeal, costing time and money.
- Provider-level utilization analytics are increasingly common. A
  practice with greater than 50 percent of procedural claims attaching
  Modifier 25 should expect Pre-Payment Reviews.
- Coder review before claim submission is the most cost-effective
  defense. A second-opinion check that scores defensibility against the
  four-criterion JARALL Standard catches weak documentation early.
