Modifier 25 indicates that on the day a procedure or service was
performed, the patient's condition required a significant, separately
identifiable Evaluation and Management service above and beyond the
usual preoperative and postoperative care.

Tips for documentation:

- The chief complaint must name a problem that is distinct from the
  procedure's primary indication. A chief complaint that reads as the
  procedure indication does not establish separability.
- Exam findings must include observations on at least one body part or
  condition that is not the procedure site. Generic statements of
  unremarkable findings are not sufficient.
- The medical decision making must reflect an independent judgment with
  risks, data review, and a management plan distinct from the procedure
  decision.
- When the E/M and procedure are at different anatomic sites, append the
  appropriate LT/RT or T1 to T9 modifier in addition to Modifier 25.
