Q: Does the decision to perform the procedure itself qualify as a
separately identifiable E/M service?

A: No. The decision-to-perform component of a minor procedure is bundled
into the procedure's pre-operative work. To support Modifier 25, the
documentation must establish that the E/M service addressed a problem
distinct from that decision.

Q: Can the same provider bill an E/M and a minor procedure on the same
day for the same patient?

A: Yes, when the documentation supports a distinct problem and the
related history, exam, and medical decision making meet the level of
service billed. Modifier 25 must be appended to the E/M service.

Q: What about same-site E/M and procedure?

A: When both services occur at the same anatomic site, the documentation
must establish a separable problem that is independent of the procedure.
Without that, the E/M service is not separately reportable.
