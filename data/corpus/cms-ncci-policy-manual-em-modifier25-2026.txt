Chapter 1, Section 3: Modifier 25 and Same-Day Minor Procedures (placeholder)

Modifier 25 is appended to an Evaluation and Management service to indicate
that on the day a procedure or service identified by another HCPCS or CPT
code was performed, the patient's condition required a significant,
separately identifiable Evaluation and Management service above and beyond
the usual preoperative and postoperative care associated with that
procedure or service. The E/M service must be supported by documentation
that meets the relevant level of service. The decision to perform a minor
procedure during the same encounter does not by itself qualify the visit
for a separate E/M service.

A separately identifiable E/M service must be supported by a distinct
problem. When the chief complaint is solely the indication for the
procedure, Modifier 25 is not appropriate. When the chief complaint
identifies an unrelated or new problem that requires evaluation, the
E/M service may be reported with Modifier 25 if the documentation
supports the level of service billed.

When the E/M and the procedure are performed on different anatomic sites,
both LT/RT or T1 through T9 modifiers should be appended to the
procedure code in addition to Modifier 25 on the E/M service. Site-
specific documentation in the encounter note is required.
